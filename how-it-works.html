How FindLoop Protects Belongings

Learn how our privacy-safe QR system connects lost items with their owners using secure campus handover points.

1

Step 01: Register Your Belonging & Private Detail

Add your calculator, bottle, bag, or ID holder to your owner dashboard. Record an optional private distinguishing mark (e.g. "small scratch on display") visible strictly to you.

2

Step 02: Print & Attach Your Unique QR Tag

FindLoop generates a printable QR tag referencing a cryptographically random token (e.g. FL-8X92K). Personal phone numbers or student IDs are never embedded in the QR code.

3

Step 03: Finder Scans QR Code (No App Needed)

When someone finds your lost item, they scan the tag using any phone camera. They land on a public web page to report where and when they found it without logging in.

4

Step 04: Collect Item via 6-Digit Handover Code

Visit the specified campus handover desk (Security Desk, Library Desk, Reception), provide the 6-digit collection code, and collect your item. Status becomes RETURNED.
