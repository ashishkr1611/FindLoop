Simple 4-Step Process

How FindLoop Works

No batteries, no app downloads for finders, zero public personal contact exposure.

01

Register your item

Create a profile for your calculator, water bottle, or backpack in under 30 seconds.

02

Attach your QR tag

Print or stick your unique FindLoop QR tag onto your belonging.

03

Someone finds & scans

A finder scans the code using any phone camera. No app download or login required.

04

You safely get it back

Receive an instant report and collect your item at a secure campus desk using a 6-digit code.

🛡️ Privacy Protection Guarantee

Your personal information stays 100% private.

Traditional lost-and-found tags print phone numbers, emails, or student IDs directly on the tag. FindLoop uses secure random QR tokens so finders never access your personal identity.

  • ✓ The finder never sees your phone number, address, or student ID.
  • ✓ Private distinguishing marks are strictly restricted to your owner account.
  • ✓ Regenerate or deactivate any tag instantly if lost or compromised.
