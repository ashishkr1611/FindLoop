Traditional tags print phone numbers or emails directly on items. FindLoop generates secure, randomized QR tokens. When a finder scans your tag, they only see the item category and a safe report form — your phone number, email, and student ID remain 100% private.

The finder submits a report specifying where the item was found and where it was handed over (e.g. Campus Security Desk or Library). You receive an instant alert on your owner dashboard with a unique 6-digit verification code to claim your item.

You can instantly regenerate or print a new QR tag for free from your owner dashboard anytime. Generating a new tag deactivates the old token immediately for complete security.
