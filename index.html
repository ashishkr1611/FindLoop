🛡️ Privacy Protection Guarantee

Your personal information stays 100% private.

Traditional lost-and-found tags print phone numbers, emails, or student IDs directly on the tag. FindLoop uses secure random QR tokens so finders never access your personal identity.

  • ✓ The finder never sees your phone number, address, or student ID.
  • ✓ Private distinguishing marks are strictly restricted to your owner account.
  • ✓ Regenerate or deactivate any tag instantly if lost or compromised.
